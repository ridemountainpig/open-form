Studio's Responses tab shows new responses as they arrive, exports what the current search and filters show, lets you pick its columns, steps through responses with the arrow keys, reads rating scales as `4 / 5`, shows cards on phones and confirms deletes in its own dialog. CSV exports give **Submitted at** in the form's time zone, checkboxes as Yes / No and languages by name.
